# AdSense Review Checklist

These changes reduce known problems. They do not promise Google approval. A
`noindex` tag is not a substitute for useful, accurate content.

## What Is In Place

- Ads remain off. Consent settings and the publisher ID were preserved.
- Title-only, missing, stale, and unchecked explanations are not treated as finished summaries.
- Those pages do not qualify for ads or homepage features. Reference-only bill pages
  have `noindex, follow` and are excluded from bill sitemaps.
- Useful Wyoming voting archives stay available, even without a plain-English summary.
- Missing source links no longer get a "Source attached" badge.
- The site explains what its automated checks can and cannot establish.
- The Minnesota feed parser and the Wyoming budget explanation were corrected.

## Owner Review Before Resubmitting

1. Confirm the public publisher name and a real person responsible for corrections.
   Do not add a made-up editor, qualification, or review date.
2. Have that person review a small Wyoming collection against the current official
   text. Check amounts, amendments, exceptions, dates, who is affected, and source links.
   Record which source version was reviewed, by whom, and when.
3. Review the guide draft in `docs/drafts/wyoming-bill-record-guide.md`. It is not
   published. Expand it with questions actual readers ask and publish only after review.
4. Test corrections and contact links from a phone. Confirm that reports reach the
   person responsible for fixing them.
5. Review actual Analytics and Search Console reports. Check useful visits, repeat
   visits, and search indexing. Do not buy traffic or create artificial visits/clicks.
6. Check a fresh sample of pages across the whole site, not only Wyoming. Ensure
   unfinished records are not presented as finished explanations or showing ads.
7. Request another AdSense review only after the content and ownership work is
   complete. Leave ads off while the site is rejected or being reviewed.

There is no invented word-count, article-count, or visitor-count target here.
More pages by themselves do not resolve a low-value-content rejection.
